Q3 of 10

What happens to reaction time as the number of stimuli increases?

lightbulb

Why?

Hick's Law states that the time it takes for a person to make a decision is a result of the possible choices he or she has: increasing the number of choices will increase the decision time logarithmically. This is why simplifying interfaces for users often leads to better conversion and faster task completion.
